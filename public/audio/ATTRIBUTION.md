## Sound effects

Sound effects are from Kenney’s CC0 packs:

- Interface Sounds — https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- Sci-Fi Sounds — https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
- Impact Sounds — https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

Kenney’s original licence texts are preserved in
`audio-source/kenney/License-*.txt`.

PIP chirps, scanner progress tick, memory/history chords, Mars wind and the
deep-space drone were synthesised for this project and are dedicated to the
public domain under CC0 1.0. PIP’s spoken lines remain the project’s existing
Kokoro recordings.
